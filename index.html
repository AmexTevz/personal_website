About me

I test web, API, mobile, and desktop software.

I am a senior QA engineer with 15+ years of experience building automation for UI, API, mobile, and backend systems. I move teams from manual-heavy testing toward suites that run in CI, and I work with engineering and product on testability before the code is finished.

I am currently a Senior Systems Test Engineer at HMSHost by Avolta in Bethesda, Maryland. Before that I automated device-management and mobile workflows at Apkudo, and earlier I tested software at Wireless Buybacks, Uber, and Samsung Electronics. Originally from Georgia, I moved to the United States with my family in 2016.

  • End-to-end frameworks with Playwright, Selenium, Pytest, and Robot Framework.
  • API coverage for REST and SOAP, plus database checks in SQL Server, PostgreSQL, and MySQL.
  • Mobile testing on Android and iOS with Appium, and desktop automation with PyWinAuto.
  • Pipelines in Jenkins and GitHub Actions, with cross-browser runs on BrowserStack.
